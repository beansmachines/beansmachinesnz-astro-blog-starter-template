# Space is within your reach

Have your own model of the sky with the Space clock
- Sun and moon move in real time, according to your location on Earth
- Moon phase changes throughout the month
- Earth lights up where it's day and night
- Sun/moon rise and set events are represented
- LEDs dim at night time for easy viewing
- Special features activated with the press of a button
- all self-contained, no internet required!
